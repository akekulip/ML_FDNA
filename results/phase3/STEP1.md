# Phase 3 / Step 1 — diagnostics (EXPLORATORY; inspected data only: train ops 0-99, test table ops 200-279)
Definitions and gates were committed first (registry/phase3_step1.yaml). Scripts: scripts/p3_step1_*.py, scripts/p3_eqinfo.py. Raw output: results/phase3/*.json.

1. **Topology (re-derived, matches the agent's counts).** 820 N-2 pairs: 677 connected, 58 gen-less-only, 83 all-gen, 2 mixed (143 islanding; 26 are new 2-cuts, 117 involve an islanding N-1 member). N-1: 38 connected, 1 gen-less-only, 2 all-gen. Base shed y0 = 0 for every control vector.
2. **G1 (islanding concentration) PASSES, but not overwhelmingly.** k=0 arm (tuned GBM, exact control, trained on N-1 only; pooled R-prec 0.867). Islanding pairs are 17.5% of rows and 29.5% of severe rows, yet hold 58.9% of missed-severe mass. Miss rate given severe: gen-less-only 26.8%, all-gen 22.0%, connected 7.4%. Connected pairs still hold 41.1% of misses, so islanding does not explain everything. Consequence per registry: Step 3 starts with island decomposition, with the convex head as the second experiment, not a replacement.
3. **Interactions (test table; tolerance 1e-6).** Braess-type (y_ab < min(y_a,y_b)) is essentially absent: 0.03% of rows at full control, 0.3% with no control. y_ab < max(y_a,y_b) is NOT rare with no control (16.1%) and ~1.1% at full control, so "N-2 >= worst N-1" is not a safe bound here. Superadditive (y_ab > y_a+y_b): 10.3% at full control (35% in gen-less-only pairs), 22.7% with no control; subadditive: 1.4% and 28.8%.
4. **Naive single-outage flow addition is not accurate** (10 ops, 6,770 connected op x pair cases): median max-branch error 2.2% of rating, 95th percentile 42%, max 264%; the overload set differs from the exact recompute in 24.1% of cases. The agent's "95% near-exact superposition" was inferred from the compensation determinant and is NOT supported as a statement about flows. Exact generalized-LODF/recompute is required, not addition.
5. **Comm-bit structure.** Under the actual state prior, the value of a fixed (op,outage) over the 14 comm bits is nearly degree-3 in failure-indicator monomials: median weighted R2 0.865 (deg<=1), 0.970 (deg<=2), 0.998 (deg<=3); N-1 and N-2 rows alike (150 sampled rows). Uniform-cube Walsh energy (constant excluded): 0.66 / 0.89 / 0.97. This is structure over comm state only; it says nothing about structure over outage combinations (registry warning), and part of it follows from control_fraction being built from CC/gateway/RTU products.
6. **FDNA motifs (current layer, best of 12 restarts, max abs error over all binary inputs).** AND2/AND3: 0.007/0.008 with hard min, 0.026/0.031 at tau 0.01, 0.125/0.146 at tau 0.05. CC AND RTU AND (GW1 OR GW2): 0.5 at every tau, i.e. the current layer cannot represent the redundant-gateway motif at all. This confirms critique point 1 concretely for units 4-7.
7. **Baseline audit (equal-information comparator A9 = Xc + raw observations + the SAME I2 per-generator probabilities, tuned on the same rows; 3 reps, n=100, screen on test table).** Mean R-precision P1 / P2: A8 0.773 / 0.804; A9 0.769 / 0.806; D_I2 0.809 / 0.835; D_I1 0.792 / 0.827; D_exact (top-64) 0.791 / 0.826. D_I2 - A9 = +0.041 / +0.028; D_I2 - A8 = +0.036 / +0.031. So A8 was not handicapped by missing information; giving the end-to-end learner raw observations and the same inference outputs does not close the gap. Also D_exact - A8 = +0.018 / +0.022 and D_I2 > D_exact by ~0.02, consistent with the earlier finding that the posterior mean of shed is not the ranking-optimal statistic.
   Caveat: screen on an inspected block, 3 reps, shared draws within a rep; not a confirmatory number.

## What this changes
- Step 2 (FDNA repair) is warranted: the OR limitation is real (motif fit fails at every tau) and hard-min was never tested. Expectation stays <= ~0.01 R-precision, not a stopping rule.
- Step 3 goes island-decomposition first (G1 passed), with the caveat that 41% of misses are in connected pairs, where the convex head or interaction modelling would matter.
- The decomposition effect survives the equal-information audit at screen level; the confirm-block claim (tier 2) is not weakened, but is still tier 2.
